For feedback/log evidence, bind every observation to the exact package/build and startup boundary; split mixed historical logs before attributing an error. Keep older failures and older positive device results attached to their own versions. Record Owner statements, source-attributed screenshot inspection, your own visual inspection, synthetic/browser results and version-bound physical-device evidence separately. Equal displayed counters are not full-save equality. A later bounded Android success may update an earlier pending snapshot without proving iOS, PC, all-device or release acceptance; preserve both dated records. / 反馈／日志每项观察绑定确切包／构建与启动边界，混合历史日志先分段再归因；旧失败与旧真机成功各归原版本。分列 Owner 陈述、来源方已看截图、本人视觉检查、合成／浏览器结果及版本绑定的真机证据。展示计数相等不等于完整存档相等。后续有界 Android 成功可更新较早的待验快照，但不证明 iOS、PC、全设备或发布验收；保留两份带日期记录。

A session-pacing implementation alone is not an automatic retry queue. Record implemented behavior and unexecuted requirements separately; do not borrow source suite counts or earlier device success as this task's results. / 仅会话节奏实现不等于自动重试队列。已实现行为与未执行要求分开，不能将来源套件数量或旧真机成功记成本轮结果。

For selected startup recovery, record the empty-only or explicitly validated auxiliary-only policy/schema, retained namespace bytes, probe/initial-commit/reopen outcomes and bounded failure stage. Keep an affected player's unobserved trigger unverified; fixture comments do not establish it. / 已选启动恢复记录全空或明确校验辅助项策略／schema、原命名空间字节保留、探针／初档提交／重开结果及有界失败阶段。受影响玩家未观察到的触发原因保持未证实，不能用替身注释定因。
